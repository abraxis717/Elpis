# CR4-P4A — bounded atomic frozen-weight transport

Explicit trusted caller selects resident panel, physical arcs, seed codes and
fuel. The sealed Geodome scheduler executes on the shadow state. Identity
transport and frozen slow-bank CGC transport are the two message policies;
CGC input is (row[0],row[1],row[2],0,1), output is its three predictions.
Destination update is the ordered mean of active incoming messages. No
semantic relation, source evidence or new training signal is inferred.

The same exclusive publication boundary as P3 applies. A failed scheduler
construction, callback, active/visit budget or other refusal poisons the
transaction; a final evidence publication refusal leaves live rows unchanged.
Explicit requested round horizon may finish RUNNING; it is not convergence.
One pass per transaction, with no mixed enrollment/adaptation. Frozen weights,
locators and authoritative records are unchanged. Only physical rows change.

Scheduler allocation occurs once per explicit staging operation and is freed
before return; no allocation-free transport claim. The sealed scheduler's
16 MiB payload ceiling is retained, and width is restricted to 3. The final
RuntimeCore integration, aggregate turn fuel, preallocated transport workspace,
rewired/flat scheduling baselines and scientific utility remain future gates.
