# Research references

This file records research papers that materially informed or contextualized
Elpis / EDEN-ECS development. These citations are scholarly acknowledgements,
not software-code provenance and not claims of endorsement by the cited authors.

## Neural quadratic / collective-variable inspiration

**Liu Ziyin, Yizhou Xu, Tomaso Poggio, Isaac Chuang.**
*Neural Quadratic Forms: A Unified Minimal Model for Sudden Learning and Scaling Laws.*
arXiv:2608.13335, 2026.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13335

This work was a direct research inspiration for the EDEN-ECS investigation of
low-dimensional collective/order-parameter descriptions of high-dimensional
neural dynamics, including the subsequent moment-state sufficiency experiments.

## Dynamical-systems context for latent reasoning

**Jeffrey Lai, Anthony Bao, John Quinn, William Gilpin.**
*Fractal basins trap latent reasoning.*
arXiv:2609.04963, 2026.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04963

This work is relevant context for Elpis investigations of attractor structure,
basins, transient dynamics, and reasoning viewed as a dynamical system. It is
listed as research context rather than source-code provenance.
